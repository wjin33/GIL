Md Badrul Hasan is a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Harold Vance Department of Petroleum Engineering at Texas A&M University. He earned his Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Maryland, Baltimore County in 2026. His research combines GPU acceleration of large-scale Fortran solvers with physics-constrained machine-learning closures for coastal and atmospheric flows, spanning coastal storm-impact modeling and meso-scale hurricane boundary layer simulation. He is the inaugural recipient of the AIAA Professor Kirti "Karman" Ghia Memorial Award (2025) and a 2026–2027 ASME Fluids Engineering Division Ghia Scholar, serving on the ASME Computational Fluid Dynamics Technical Committee (CFDTC). He serves as a reviewer for Physics of Fluids, the AIAA Aviation Forum, and ASME FEDSM.
