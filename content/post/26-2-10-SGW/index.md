Nicolas Cardenas Acevedo presented his research on modeling microseismicity in enhanced geothermal systems and the impact of injection parameters at Utah FORGE at the 2026 Stanford Geothermal Workshop.

<!--more-->
His simulation work shows that the maximum magnitude of induced seismicity does not increase with injection rate when a standard Mohr-Coulomb friction model is used for pre-existing fractures. The findings prompted discussion with the microseismicity community.
