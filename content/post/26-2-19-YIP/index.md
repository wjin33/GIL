Dr. Wencheng Jin received a prestigious [Young Investigator Program (YIP)](https://www.onr.navy.mil/2026-young-investigators) award from the Office of Naval Research. The Texas A&M College of Engineering also featured the project in “[New model seeks to predict coastal changes in real time](https://news.engineering.tamu.edu/news/2026/04/08/new-model-seeks-to-predict-costal-changes-in-real-time/).”

<!--more-->
In this project, Dr. Jin and his team will explore GPU acceleration, learned correction and interpolation, and emulators to accelerate XBeach for real-time forecasting of coastal hydromorphodynamics during extreme events.
