GIL is contributing to a new ARPA-E ROCKS project that will develop an advanced drilling system for faster, real-time identification of critical minerals.

<!--more-->

The $3.5 million project, **Rapid Analysis of Precious and Targeted Ores with Rotary Drilling (RAPTOR)**, is led by Dr. Roman Shor at Texas A&M University. Dr. Wencheng Jin serves as a co-principal investigator on the multi-institutional team.

GIL's role is to develop **machine-learning-assisted mineralogy profiling from drill-cuttings analysis**, enabling mineral composition to be estimated in real time as samples reach the surface. This capability will help connect cutting measurements with rapid subsurface characterization and support faster, data-driven decisions during mineral exploration.

The project will support GIL Ph.D. student [Xiaoyang Du](https://wjin33.github.io/GIL/author/xiaoyang-du/), whose research focuses on multimodal subsurface characterization and physics-constrained machine learning for rapid lithology and mineral-composition estimation.
